Delete My Account

To request deletion of your account and associated data from [Mimilogs], follow these steps:

Method 1: Email Request

Send an email to mimilogsns@gmail.com with:

  • Subject: "Delete My Account"
  • Your registered email address or phone number

We will process your request within 7 business days.

Method 2: In-App Request

Open the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete Account.

What Data is Deleted?

All data stored in Firebase, including:

  • Email address / Phone number
  • User preferences
  • Activity history
